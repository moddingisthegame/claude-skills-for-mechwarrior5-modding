# Mod Editor EULA — key terms

Source: `MW5_Mod_Editor_EULA.pdf` (dated 2019-12-13) —
http://static.mw5mercs.com/docs/MW5_Mod_Editor_EULA.pdf

Full text runs 10 pages of standard ToS boilerplate; this is what a
modder actually needs before creating/distributing mods.

- **"Editor"** = the Unreal Editor (governed by Epic's own UE EULA,
  https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/eula/publishing). **"Licensed
  Materials"** = the MW5 Mercs game Software/Content, made available under
  this agreement. **"Mod"** = content built with the Editor + Licensed
  Materials to customize the MW5 Mercs gameplay experience.
- **License grant:** personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable,
  non-sublicensable, for the sole purpose of creating MW5 Mercs Mods for use
  with the MW5 Mercs videogame. Grants **no title/ownership** in the
  Licensed Materials.
- **Conditions:** you may not distribute/sublicense Licensed Materials
  except bundled for use with MW5 Mercs and **only if you charge no fee**;
  you may not build or distribute standalone games/products from the
  Licensed Materials or Editor; other users may use/modify/distribute your
  Mods under this same license.
- **Prohibited:** commercial use, resale/rental/licensing of Licensed
  Materials, removing security tech, IP/rights infringement, unlawful
  export.
- **Your Content:** Piranha doesn't claim ownership of content you create,
  but you grant them a royalty-free license to use/distribute it as needed
  to operate the service, and you indemnify them against claims arising
  from your content (e.g. IP infringement, unlawful content).
- **Feedback you give them becomes their property.**
- Trademarks: MECHWARRIOR® is a Microsoft Corp. trademark used under
  license; PIRANHA GAMES is Piranha Games Inc.'s; EPIC/UNREAL marks are
  Epic's — all used under license, not implying endorsement of third-party
  use.
- **Governing law:** British Columbia, Canada. **Limitation period:** any
  claim must be brought within 1 year. DMCA takedown process is specified
  (contact `support@mw5mercs.com`).
- Piranha can amend the agreement, suspend/terminate access at their sole
  discretion, and disclaims guarantees on internet-dependent service
  quality.
